Standards

Editorial Policy and Corrections

Last updated: October 2026

Nexora News aims to publish news that is accurate, clearly written and fair. This page explains the standards we work to and what we do when we get something wrong.

Accuracy

We aim to report facts accurately and to check key details before we publish. Where information is unconfirmed, we say so. We try to distinguish clearly between what is known, what is claimed and what is opinion.

Sources

  • We rely on reliable sources, such as official statements, public records, established news organisations and people with direct knowledge.
  • Where it helps the reader, we name or describe our sources.
  • We do not copy other publishers' work. Where we draw on another organisation's reporting, we say so and write our own account.

Independence and Fairness

Our editorial decisions are made independently. We aim to be fair to the people and organisations we write about and, where relevant, to represent more than one point of view.

Opinion and Analysis

Where an article contains opinion or analysis, we aim to make this clear to the reader and to keep it separate from straight news reporting.

Images and Video

We aim to use images and video that we have the right to use, and to describe them accurately. If you believe we have used your work without permission, please contact us and we will review it promptly.

Corrections and Updates

We want to put mistakes right quickly. If we find or are told about a significant error, we will correct it and, where the change is important, add a note to the article explaining what was changed.

To report an error or request a correction, email contact@nexoranews.uk with a link to the article and a description of the problem. You can also use our Contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy are welcome. Please reach us through our Contact page.
