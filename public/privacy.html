Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nexora News respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit and use the Nexora News website.

1. Information We Collect

Nexora News may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using certain features of the website.

For example, if you subscribe to our newsletter, we may collect your email address so that we can send you requested updates.

2. Newsletter Subscriptions

If you choose to subscribe to the Nexora News newsletter, your email address may be stored securely for the purpose of sending newsletter communications.

You can choose to stop receiving newsletter communications at any time.

3. Website Usage Information

We may use technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location and pages visited to understand how visitors use the website and improve our services.

This information may be collected through analytics or similar technologies if such services are enabled.

4. Cookies

Nexora News may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, remember preferences and understand website usage.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Third-Party Services

Nexora News may use third-party services to provide website functionality, analytics, hosting, authentication, newsletter services or other technical features.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising

Nexora News may display advertising in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide relevant advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Any advertising services used on the website will be subject to the applicable policies of those providers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexora News is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of certain information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Nexora News handles information, please contact us through our Contact page.
